About

Where language meets data—turning raw audio into the intelligence that powers the next generation of voice AI.

I'm a Data Engineer and Data Ops Lead working at Meta through TEKsystems in Vancouver. I build speech data pipelines, organize human evaluation, and turn model-quality results into decisions research and product teams can use.

My path began with language and learning design at Duolingo, followed by graduate study in data science and an NLP internship at Seasalt.ai. Today, that combination helps me connect the engineering behind a voice system with how people actually experience it.

I hold a Master of Data Science in Computational Linguistics from UBC. I work across Chinese, Korean, English and Portuguese, and bring that multilingual perspective to evaluation design, annotation quality and error analysis.
